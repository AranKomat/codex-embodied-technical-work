## First repository-grade evaluation

The frozen program was graded through `eval/scripts/run_grade.py` with
`assembly.server_repair.franka.osc`, seed `0`, and `num_envs=3`. The
authoritative result is archived under `artifacts/r1_frozen_grade_seed0/`:

- success rate: `3/3`;
- score mean/min/max: `1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0`;
- score standard deviation: `0.0`;
- all three trajectories satisfied grasped, aligned, and pressed criteria;
- solve wall time: `676.409 s`.

The matched seed-1 grade is also archived under
`artifacts/r1_frozen_grade_seed1/`:

- success rate: `3/3`;
- score mean/min/max: `1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0`;
- score standard deviation: `0.0`;
- solve wall time: `685.948 s`.

Across seeds 0 and 1, the frozen program therefore passed `6/6` grader
trajectories with score mean `1.0` and no observed variance.

This confirms that the frozen controller survives the grader's three parallel
randomized trajectories for this seed. It is stronger than a replay-only check,
but it is still one seed and one fixture/fault family.

This is evidence of initial robustness, but not yet a complete R1 campaign. The
three environments are randomized trajectories in the same fixture family; they
do not yet cover a different faulty component, deliberate staging/layout
offsets, or a different initial robot configuration. Those are the next R1
variants. Keep the program frozen while evaluating them and report each variant
separately.
